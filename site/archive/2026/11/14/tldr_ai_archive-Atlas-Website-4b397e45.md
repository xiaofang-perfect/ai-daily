# Atlas

Next generation world model

Generate and reconstruct worlds and bring creations into your own workflows with our tools and developer APIs.

[Try Atlas](https://atlas.worldlabs.ai)

## Build with Atlas

### Start from your images

Turn photos or generated images into explorable worlds. Viewfinder reconstructs the scene and lets you move the camera through it.

### Shape the result

Block out a scene in Chisel to guide its structure. Move things around and see every camera view update.

### Bring worlds into your workflow

Convert splats into meshes with real geometry. Take your worlds into games, simulations, and the 3D tools you already use.

## Tools launching with Atlas

- Reconstruct a scene from photos or generated images, explore new viewpoints and render camera paths.
- Shape a world through controllable scene building and grayboxing.
- Convert splats into meshes for games and interactive environments.
- Explore new ways to light and manipulate splats.
- Bring Atlas capabilities into your own applications and existing tools, including Blender.

## Atlas across industries

### Film & VFX

Build a set, direct the camera through it and render the shots you need.

### Architecture & Design

Shape a space from photos or a graybox scene, explore it from any viewpoint and take a mesh into your tools.

### Robotics

Create spatial environments from real or generated scenes for robotics research and simulation workflows.
