We are seeing a bunch of new outputs being circulated on X, and testers believe they're from Claude Opus 5.6. Of course, Anthropic hasn't announced anything, and not everyone is labeling this as Opus 5.6, but the results look like a step up from Opus 5.5.

In mid-October, some Opus 5.5 requests [appeared to be landing on an unannounced model](https://www.testingcatalog.com/anthropic-tests-new-model-opus-5-5-routing-opus-5-6-fable-5-5/), which could have been an early Opus 5.6 or [Fable 5.5](https://www.testingcatalog.com/anthropic-several-models-coming-fable-5-5-reaches-partners/). Fable 5.5 never formally launched. Anthropic instead [released Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2 on November 9](https://www.testingcatalog.com/anthropic-launches-claude-fable-5-2-and-mythos-5-2/).

That said, things are heating up again. X user Leo said it's likely being tested with a subset of Claude users, some in Claude Code, some in Chat, and some not at all, and called it "quite a jump over Opus 5.5."

Meanwhile, tipster Chetaslua posted that their run was routed from "Opus 5.5 Extra" and shared a Claude Code screenshot as proof, along with the [code and prompt on GitHub](https://github.com/ChetasLua/the-last-page?ref=testingcatalog.com). The Last Page is a hand-drawn-style short about a small fox, which they first shared as something "Opus 5.6 made... in pure code." There's no denying that the output looks incredible.

Another user named Legit labeled it "Opus-next" and compared the result with Fable 5.2 and allegedly Fable 5.5 as well. All three are SVGs of a rainy bamboo forest. We can't verify they actually had Fable 5.5 or Opus 5.6, for that matter, so treat that label as one tester's claim.

These are early, cherry-picked outputs, with no official confirmation that it is Opus 5.6. [OpenAI launched GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol](https://www.testingcatalog.com/openai-launches-gpt-6-2-astra-and-sol-on-chatgpt-and-apis/) on November 17, and [Google shipped Gemini 4 Neon a day later](https://www.testingcatalog.com/google-launches-gemini-4-neon-on-gemini-antigravity-and-apis/). If you're on Opus 5.5 Extra, you might already be trying it.
