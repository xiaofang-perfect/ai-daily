A coalition of [42 state attorneys general](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/25/state-ags-fight-character-ais-plan-to-auction-off-users-private-chats/) is [trying to prevent](https://oag.ca.gov/system/files/attachments/press-docs/characterai.pdf) Character.AI from [selling off user data](https://www.theverge.com/policy/1011417/new-york-ag-characterai-bankruptcy) as it navigates Chapter 11 bankruptcy proceedings. In a [deluge](https://oag.maryland.gov/News/pages/Attorney-General-Brown-Challenges-Plan-to-Auction-Off-Character.AI-Users%E2%80%99-Private-Chats%2C-Including-Kids%E2%80%99-Data%2C-to-Highest-B.aspx) of [nearly](https://portal.ct.gov/ag/press-releases/2026-press-releases/attorney-general-tong-challenges-plan-to-auction-off-character-ai-private-chats) [identical](https://oag.ca.gov/news/press-releases/attorney-general-bonta-opposes-asset-sale-characterai-amid-child-safety-concerns) [press](https://www.ag.state.mn.us/Office/Communications/2026/11/23_CharacterAI.asp) releases, the various AGs accuse the company of planning to auction off “the private chat histories of more than 21 million U.S. accounts — more than 2.1 million of them registered to minors — with no safeguards for the people whose conversations are being sold.”

# 42 state AGs are trying to stop Character.AI from selling users’ chats

A bipartisan coalition of AGs say Character.AI is planning to sell the personal data of minors to the highest bidder.

In October, Character.AI [filed for bankruptcy](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1009802/character-ai-files-for-bankruptcy-disney) following [years](https://www.theverge.com/report/841610/ai-chatbot-suicide-safety-failure) of [scandals](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1009463/character-ai-chats-encouraging-self-harm-detailed-in-unredacted-kentucky-lawsuit) and [legal challenges](https://www.theverge.com/news/858102/characterai-google-teen-suicide-settlement), including from [Disney](https://www.theverge.com/news/789451/its-curtains-for-ai-elsa-spider-man-and-darth-vader). Disney ended up buying some of the company’s assets and [hiring its CEO](https://www.theverge.com/entertainment/997555/karandeep-anand-disney-character-ai), Karandeep Anand, in a sort of “acquihire” arrangement that will also see “a number of Character.AI’s technical team” join the entertainment giant. The Disney deal also faced opposition from a similarly broad coalition of AGs.

The main point of contention with Character.AI’s plan to sell its chat data is that it offers no protections for users. The AGs say there are no requirements that the buyer honor existing privacy policies, no avenues for users to delete their data, and no inherent limits on what a buyer could do with that data, including selling it on to yet another party. It doesn’t help that the first bidder has a limited paper trail, having only been established on November 13th, 2026, with no parent company or investors listed.

Some states, including New York, Connecticut, California, and Colorado, have also passed laws regulating chatbots that Character.AI may be in violation of when they go into effect on January 1st, 2027.
