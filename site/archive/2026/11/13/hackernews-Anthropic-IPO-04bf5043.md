## How to participate

## FAQs

- ## What is the timeline for the IPO? The IPO process begins with a roadshow starting on November 10th (EST). Subject to regulatory clearance, the final IPO share price is expected to be set on November 18th (EST), and shares are expected to begin publicly trading on November 19th (EST).
- ## What is Anthropic offering in this IPO? Anthropic is offering shares of its Class A common stock to the public, which will trade on Nasdaq under the ticker symbol “ANT”.
- ## How can I participate in the IPO? You can request shares through a participating brokerage platform. To participate, you must have an active account and submit an indication of interest through their platform.
- ## Am I eligible to participate in the Anthropic IPO? Your brokerage account or investing app will confirm whether you’re eligible when you submit your request to participate.
- ## At what price will the shares be offered? The final IPO price is expected to be determined on November 18th (EST), based on regulatory clearance, investor demand and market conditions.
- ## When will I know if I received shares of Anthropic? Your brokerage account or investing app will confirm your allocation on the first day of trading, which is expected to be November 19th (EST).
- ## What is the difference between buying in the IPO and buying after the stock begins trading? Shares bought in the IPO will be purchased at the offering price, which is expected to be set on November 18th (EST). Shares bought after Anthropic starts trading will be purchased at the prevailing market price at that time.
- ## Will I receive all the shares I apply for? There is no guarantee that any investor will receive all or any of the shares they apply for in the Anthropic IPO. However, you should be ready to purchase all the shares for which you apply.
- ## What is the prospectus? A prospectus is a regulatory filing that provides detailed information about Anthropic’s business, financial statements, risk factors, management team, use of proceeds, offering details, among other items. Investors should review the prospectus closely before investing.
