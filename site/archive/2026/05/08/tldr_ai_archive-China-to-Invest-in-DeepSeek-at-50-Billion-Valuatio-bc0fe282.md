China’s DeepSeek is raising money from government-backed investors, aligning the artificial-intelligence startup with Beijing’s push for technology self-sufficiency.

Some prospective investors have valued DeepSeek at around $50 billion in recent talks, people familiar with the matter said. The valuation has surged in recent weeks after [previous discussions](https://www.wsj.com/tech/ai/chinas-deepseek-looks-to-tap-external-investors-including-alibaba-tencent-4fcb607d?mod=article_inline) envisioned a range between $10 billion and $30 billion.
