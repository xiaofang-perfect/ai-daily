[Alphabet](https://www.cnbc.com/quotes/GOOG/) shares sank 4% on Thursday following a report that the company has delayed releasing its flagship [artificial intelligence](https://www.cnbc.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/) model.

The search giant's Gemini 3.5 Pro AI model is months behind schedule due to the company's efforts to improve its performance, according to [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-16/google-gemini-launch-delayed-as-tech-falls-short-of-internal-goals), citing sources familiar with the matter. The model's coding capabilities, in particular, were short of internal expectations and come at a time when rivals like [OpenAI](https://www.cnbc.com/quotes/OPENAI.FG/) and [Meta](https://www.cnbc.com/quotes/META/) have recently debuted new AI models that outpace Google's current offerings in generating software code, the report said.

The company previously announced the[ Gemini 3.5 Pro AI model](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/19/google-ai-ultra-gemini-spark-omni.html) in May as part of the company's annual Google I/O developer conference, saying at the time that it was being used internally, but wouldn't be ready for a broader rollout until the following month.

An Alphabet spokesperson told CNBC in an emailed statement that the company is "shipping quickly across a wide range of models while keeping them highly cost-effective for customers."

"We're currently testing 3.5 Pro, an upgraded Flash model, and other models with partners, and we're productively engaged with the U.S. government," the spokesperson said.

Code-generation has become one of the biggest use cases for AI model providers like [Anthropic](https://www.cnbc.com/quotes/ANTHR.FG/) and OpenAI and Chinese AI labs like [Z.ai](https://www.cnbc.com/quotes/2513-HK/) that offer[ so-called open-weight variants](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/01/26/american-ai-coding-agents-are-impressive-but-so-are-chinas.html) that developers can access for free via the open-source ecosystem.

Meta debuted last week its[ Muse Spark 1.1 AI model](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/09/meta-jumps-into-ai-coding-market-to-chase-anthropic-and-openai.html), which the company's AI chief Alexandr Wang described as the social media giant's "strongest model for agentic and coding work yet."

OpenAI last week released its[ GPT-5.6 Sol](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/08/openai-expanding-gpt-5point6-ai-model-release-ending-government-limits.html) AI model, which CEO [Sam Altman](https://www.cnbc.com/sam-altman/) said is 54% more token efficient on[ agentic coding tasks](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/09/open-ai-sam-altman-chatgpt-5-6-sol.html), underscoring how AI labs are pitching their respective AI coding models as being cost-effective relative to their performance.
