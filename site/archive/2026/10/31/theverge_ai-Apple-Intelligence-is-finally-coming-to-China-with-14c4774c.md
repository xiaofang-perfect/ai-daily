Apple will launch Apple Intelligence in China with iOS 27.3, the company announced [on its Chinese newsroom website](https://www.apple.com.cn/newsroom/2026/10/apple-intelligence-features-are-coming-to-users-in-china/), as [reported by *MacRumors*](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/10/30/apple-says-ios-27-3-beta-coming-next-week/). The Apple Intelligence features will be available first in an iOS 27.3 beta that’s set to launch on November 4th, with a full iOS 27.3 release scheduled for December.

# Apple Intelligence is finally coming to China with iOS 27.3

Apple is launching an iOS 27.3 beta next week that will bring some of the initial Apple Intelligence features to the Chinese market.

This launch doesn’t bring the new Siri AI to China. Instead, Apple is launching several of its earlier Apple Intelligence features like notification summaries, real-time translation, rewriting and proofreading tools, visual intelligence, and removing objects from photos. Apple also says it has worked with Ailbaba to add support for its Qwen chatbot in some experiences, which sounds similar to iOS’s integration [with OpenAI’s ChatGPT](https://www.theverge.com/2024/12/11/24317119/ios-18-2-apple-intelligence-chatgpt-genmoji) that launched in 2024.

Apple Intelligence was approved in China [in July](https://www.theverge.com/tech/965872/apple-intelligence-approved-in-china), which signalled that the launch of the AI tools was getting near. Apple trained its own LLM specifically for China, [*Reuters* reported in August](https://www.reuters.com/business/retail-consumer/apple-trains-its-own-ai-model-china-market-with-alibabas-support-sources-say-2026-08-14/).
