Starting on October 9th, anyone using Google Gemini on a free plan will be [limited to the Flash Lite model](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/17004136). Free users can currently choose from Gemini Flash Lite, Flash, and Pro, but now you’ll need a $4.99/month Google AI Plus subscription to access the standard Flash model. However, that subscription is also getting a downgrade – it will soon no longer include Gemini Pro. Google says AI Plus subscribers will receive an email letting them know when they’ll lose access to it.

# Google is about to remove free access to Gemini Flash and Pro

Users on Google’s $4.99 AI Plus subscription are also losing access to Gemini Pro.

After these changes, access to the top-tier Gemini Pro model and a “Deep Think” advanced reasoning option will be restricted to users with a Google AI Pro or Ultra subscription, which cost $19.99 or $99.99 per month, respectively.

Google says users will still be able to choose from low, medium, and high effort levels for each model, but using higher effort levels could [burn through your Gemini usage limit](https://www.theverge.com/news/773496/google-gemini-usage-limits) faster.
