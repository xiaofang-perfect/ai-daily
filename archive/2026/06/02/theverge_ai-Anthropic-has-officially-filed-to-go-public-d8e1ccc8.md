After months of speculation about whether OpenAI or Anthropic would be first in their race to IPO, Anthropic on Monday reached a key milestone: filing to kick off the process with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission.

The filing sets the stage for what’s sure to be a massive IPO. As of its fundraise last week, Anthropic is [being called](https://go.skimresources.com/?id=1025X1701640&xs=1&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.morningstar.com%2Fstocks%2Fanthropic-bests-openai-valuation-race-hitting-965b) the world’s most valuable startup, with a post-money valuation of $965 billion. That tops the $852 billion post-money valuation of OpenAI, which is its biggest rival.

Anthropic chose to submit its draft registration statement to the SEC confidentially, according to a [blog post](https://www.anthropic.com/news/confidential-draft-s1-sec) from the company, meaning key details typically included in that form about a company’s operations — like potential risks to its business, executive compensation, and other financials — won’t become public until later on in the process.

The news comes less than two weeks before SpaceX’s planned June 12 IPO. Elon Musk’s SpaceX — which owns xAI and also has a newly minted deal with Anthropic that has the latter paying $15 billion per year to use SpaceX data centers — is currently set to raise $80 billion in its public debut, which would make it the largest IPO in history.

The filing also comes shortly after OpenAI [came out ahead](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/932383/jury-verdict-musk-v-altman-openai-trial) in its high-profile legal battle with Musk, when a judge dismissed all claims due to the statute of limitations.
