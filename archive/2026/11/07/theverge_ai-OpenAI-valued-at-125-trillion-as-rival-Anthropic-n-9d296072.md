OpenAI is now valued at $1.25 trillion in a new round of funding that has raised another $11 billion, according to [*Bloomberg*](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-06/openai-completes-11-billion-portion-of-funding-at-1-25-trillion-value). That would make it one of the world’s most valuable companies, not long after raising $122 billion from investors in March at a valuation of $852 billion.

# OpenAI valued at $1.25 trillion, as rival Anthropic nears its IPO

CEO Sam Altman said recently that OpenAI is prioritizing safety instead of going ‘guns blazing’ toward an IPO.

Echoing *[FT](https://www.ft.com/content/7a3ad950-1b3b-4d9e-8c15-65a61ae6180f?syn-25a6b1a6=1)* and [*Wall Street Journal*](https://www.wsj.com/tech/ai/openai-raises-less-than-half-of-30-billion-target-in-latest-round-91aecdc5) reports from last week, *Bloomberg* says OpenAI is still pursuing additional investments that could increase the amount to $20 billion, and that it had initially hoped to raise as much as $30 billion at a higher valuation of $1.4 trillion. That was before tech stocks dropped, and the results of the midterms showed how much public opinion has turned [against AI companies and data center projects](https://www.theverge.com/policy/1026365/data-centers-midterms-results).

OpenAI’s rival Anthropic [filed its S-1 to go public](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1018476/anthropic-ipo-details-s1-filed-sec) last week. Earlier this year, OpenAI CEO Sam Altman said it’s [holding off on its own IPO](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1002505/sam-altman-openai-ipo-devday-ai-safety) until it can make more confident claims about its models’ safety. He didn’t give a specific timeline for the IPO, but said at the time, “I think it’s also kind of bad for the world if OpenAI waits too long to go public.”
