As backlash grows over Flock’s AI surveillance cameras, Texas Governor Greg Abbott has [frozen state spending](https://www.texastribune.org/2026/08/28/texas-greg-abbott-flock-cameras-order-state-money/) on them. The move came just ahead of the publication of a [*Texas Tribune* investigation](https://www.texastribune.org/2026/08/28/texas-flock-cameras-auto-insurance-fee-mvcpa-grants/) that revealed the state spent over $30 million on Flock cameras. That money was primarily raised by tacking a $1 fee onto insurance policies, allegedly to help fight catalytic converter theft.

Flock cameras have increasingly drawn ire from [across the political spectrum](https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/2026/08/29/flock-cameras-stir-bipartisan-fury-gov-greg-abbott-blocks-them-texas/) as they’ve become the subject of privacy concerns. There have been multiple incidents of officers being placed on leave or [criminally charged](https://www.404media.co/cops-keep-getting-arrested-for-using-flock-to-stalk-people/) for misusing Flock systems, including at least six in Texas alone. The company has also come under scrutiny for its [data-sharing](https://www.wired.com/story/how-an-atlanta-suburb-ended-up-sharing-flock-data-with-more-than-2000-organizations/), the placement of some of its cameras [near](https://www.wsj.com/tech/flock-cameras-are-spreading-across-america-the-backlash-is-growing-just-as-fast-fff74ed2) [playgrounds](https://www.reddit.com/r/Pflugerville/comments/1r17ys9/flock_camera_watching_playground_we_need_to/), its own [employees’ access](https://www.wral.com/news/investigates/audits-raise-questions-about-access-to-raleigh-flock-cameras-august-2026/) to video feeds, [unsecured video feeds](https://www.theverge.com/news/849624/flock-ai-camera-feeds-exposed-benn-jordan), and its ham-fisted attempts to [avoid transparency](https://youtu.be/GQoQkeMdvQM).

In recent months, cities around the country have been canceling their contracts with Flock, including many in Texas. Abbott’s Democratic opponent in the race for governor, Gina Hinojosa, has [criticized him](https://youtu.be/81IMtQ5hnYA) for expanding use of the cameras in the state. And Republican representative Keith Self recently [posted on Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/p/DbYffZ6Sy96/) that, “From Flock cameras to kill switches, we will NOT surrender our Fourth Amendment rights to the growing surveillance state.” Cheering on the [destruction of Flock cameras](https://www.theverge.com/tech/985155/flock-camera-destruction-vigilantes) has even become a unifying pastime on the internet.
