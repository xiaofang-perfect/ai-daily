Nvidia’s predicting it will pull in $108 billion in revenue within just a few months. It wouldn’t be the first company to rake in over $100 billion in quarterly revenue — Amazon, Apple, and [Alphabet](https://fortune.com/2026/07/22/anthropic-spacex-investments-google-earnings-biggest-ever-profit-quarter/) have repeatedly reached the milestone.

# Nvidia is about to be a hundred-billion-dollar-a-quarter company

The chip company made over $96 billion in the past quarter, most of it from its data center business.

Nvidia said in its [latest earnings report](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-announces-financial-results-for-second-quarter-fiscal-2027) that it brought in a record $96.2 billion in overall revenue in the past quarter, a jump of over $10 billion from the previous quarter. Its data center revenue alone more than doubled year-over-year to a record $89 billion, and the company’s profits more than doubled to $59.7 billion.

*Nvidia’s overall revenue doubled year-over-year.*

Nvidia’s “edge computing” category, which [includes its consumer gaming business](https://www.theverge.com/tech/935026/nvidia-is-officially-no-longer-a-gaming-company-in-financial-earnings), accounted for just $7.2 billion of its revenue last quarter. That’s a 27 percent increase year-over-year, but Nvidia [acknowledged](https://s201.q4cdn.com/141608511/files/doc_financials/2027/Q227/Q2FY27-CFO-Commentary.pdf) that it experienced “slower consumer PC sales that were tempered by elevated memory and systems prices.” Component shortages continue to [drive up prices for Nvidia’s consumer GPUs](https://www.theverge.com/games/973173/asus-rog-rtx-5080-price-hike-best-buy), and it also warned of [price hikes for its AI chips](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/983638/nvidias-ai-chips-are-about-to-get-more-expensive-too) ahead of Wednesday’s earnings report.
