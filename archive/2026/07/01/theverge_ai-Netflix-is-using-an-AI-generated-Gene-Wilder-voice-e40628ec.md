*Deadline*[ElevenLabs](/news/818470/elevenlabs-iconic-voice-marketplace-ai-audio) with consent from Wilder’s family, after working on productions re-creating the voice of Michael Caine and Stan Lee. It also [continues Netflix’s 2021 partnership](/22688734/netflix-roald-dahl-deal-kids-programming-competition-disney-plus) with the Roald Dahl company, and is separate from the *Charlie vs. the Chocolate Factory* animated feature that will arrive in 2027.
