Microsoft is working on an AI “super app” that combines Copilot’s chat, coding, and agentic capabilities. During an earnings call on Wednesday, Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella said the app will span “both consumer and commercial experiences” when it launches this year.

“Copilot is evolving rapidly from chat to Cowork to Autopilots,” Nadella said. “This quarter, we are bringing these Copilot experiences together, including code, in one super app… This is a major step forward, and I look forward to sharing more soon.”

Nadella’s confirmation comes months after a report from [ Fortune revealed that Microsoft](https://fortune.com/2026/05/29/microsoft-working-on-super-app/) is working on a super app that blends its Copilot chatbot, GitHub Copilot coding assistant, Copilot Cowork, and its agentic Autopilot system. Along with Microsoft, OpenAI recently 

[revealed its new ChatGPT Work app](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/963464/openai-gpt-5-6-codex-chatgpt-work), which combines ChatGPT and its AI coding tool Codex.

Microsoft’s [revenue jumped to $90 billion](https://www.theverge.com/tech/972738/xbox-revenue-microsoft-earnings-q4-2026) over the past quarter, with its AI and cloud businesses driving the increase.
